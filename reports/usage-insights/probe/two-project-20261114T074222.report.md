### How each metric is computed

- `execution.durationMs` (ms, mean, per evidence layer): Mean of the duration the producing system recorded on the outcome. Not a queue time.
- `interventions.acceptances` (count, sum, product records): Distinct \`acceptance.recorded\` facts recorded on observed works.
- `interventions.answers` (count, sum, product records): Distinct \`runtime.answer.observed\` facts recorded on observed works.
- `interventions.questions` (count, sum, product records): Distinct \`question.opened\` facts recorded on observed works.
- `interventions.total` (count, sum, product records): Distinct traceable human-interaction facts (questions, answers, acceptances) per work.
- `outcomes.accepted` (count, sum, per evidence layer): Distinct outcomes whose recorded acceptance is exactly \`accepted\`.
- `outcomes.count` (count, sum, per evidence layer): Distinct accepted-for-analysis execution outcomes per evidence layer.
- `queue.waitMs` (ms, mean, per evidence layer): Mean of explicit queued-at to dispatched-at pairs bound to the same attempt.
- `review.activeMs` (ms, mean, per evidence layer): Mean observed UI-visibility proxy interval. Partial visibility proxy only, never human hours.
- `review.readyToAcceptanceMs` (ms, mean, per evidence layer): Mean ReviewTiming.readyToAcceptanceMs, checked against its own ready/accepted timestamps.
- `usage.inputTokens` (tokens, sum, per evidence layer): Sum of known input tokens. Unknown usage is counted separately, never as zero.
- `usage.outputTokens` (tokens, sum, per evidence layer): Sum of known output tokens. Unknown usage is counted separately, never as zero.
